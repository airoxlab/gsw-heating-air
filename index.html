California requires a contractor's license for any HVAC job over $500 in labor and materials. Plenty of people working on Lake County systems do not hold one — and a botched install shows up as a wrecked compressor or a gas fault later.

  • Active C-20 license — the classification specific to heating and air conditioning.
  • $25,000 contractor's bond on file with the state.
  • Workers' compensation insurance carried for our crew — many one-person outfits carry none, which leaves the homeowner exposed.
  • Free estimates — know the price before any work begins.
