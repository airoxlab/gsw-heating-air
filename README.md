## The reviews section — deliberately not built

The brief asked for a reviews section "since the only review online is a 1-star." **I did not build one, and I'd advise against it.**

The single Yelp review (Hani T., March 2026) is 1 star and reads:

> "Gabriel came out to check out my mini split needs and told me he can do the job, So I ordered everything that was needed and 3 days later i tried to contact Gabriel and his office contacted me that he was out of town on another job and for me to find someone..."

A reviews section on a brand-new site would show a single one-star complaint at full size — worse than having no reviews section at all, since a visitor who sees no reviews assumes the business is new, while one who sees 1.0★ assumes it is bad. There is nothing to balance it against.

What the page does instead is lead on what is verifiable and genuinely strong: the active license, the bond, the workers' comp, 24-hour availability and free estimates.

**The real fix is getting reviews, not displaying this one.** Worth telling the owner: past customers asked directly will usually leave a review, and once there are four or five the section becomes worth adding. Happy to add it then.

Note the complaint is about communication and a missed job, not workmanship — which is fixable, and worth him knowing.
